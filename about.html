About the Site

This website is a portfolio and shop for my artwork. Fifty percent of all profits made here are donated to the Palestine Children’s Relief Fund and Sadagaat-USA. The remaining profits cover the cost of materials used to create each piece. You can find more information about these organizations below.

Some pieces shown—especially some of those in the Gallery tab—are not for sale. However, most can be recreated or commissioned. If you’re interested in a custom piece, please reach out using the contact information listed under the Contact tab. If you see a piece that is for sale but doesn’t quite meet your criteria, I’m always happy to discuss variations or customizations.

At the moment, custom ceramic pieces are not available, as I do not have access to a ceramic studio space for the foreseeable future. The items listed in the Ceramics section of the Shop tab are the only ceramic works currently offered.

I built this website from scratch using HTML and CSS, so the shop system is relatively simple. For instance, I don’t have a credit-card processor—I chose not to pay for a subscription to a website-building or payment processing service so that as much profit as possible can be donated. Instead, I coordinate payment directly with clients through email. Available payment methods include, Venmo, PayPal, or cash, depending on what works best for you.

To purchase a piece, click the “Claim This Piece!” button on any product page. When you click the button:

1. A short set of instructions will appear, explaining payment and shipping options.

2. After reading them, click "I understand."

3. Your device will then open an email addressed to me, with the piece you are interested in purchasing already listed in the subject line. You can describe which payment and shipping options best work for you, and I will work to accommodate these preferences.

Once I receive your message, I’ll follow up to confirm payment, delivery, or pickup details. If anything about this system is confusing—or you run into technical issues—please reach out through the Contact tab. I’m always happy to help.

About the Artist

HI, I'M AMIRA QIDWAI

(A-mee-ra Kid-why)

I am an artist and incoming law student at the University of Minnesota. My work explores the intersection of art with international political issues, geography, and human development, drawing on my experience with a wide range of media.

I was born and raised in Iowa City, Iowa. My identity as an Iowan, a Midwesterner, and a half-White, half-Pakistani woman continues to shape the themes I explore. Much of my early work focused on my heritage but as I studies Pakistani culture, I became increasingly interested in the shared threads across South Asian cultures.

Over time, this curiosity expanded into a broader focus on global connections—how cultures relate, diverge, and include one another, especially across developing regions.

I recently graduated from the University of Iowa, where I study international relations, Geographic Information Science (GIS), and Arabic. In Fall 2026, I will begin my Juris Doctorate at the University of Minnesota in Minneapolis.

The subjects I study continually inform my artistic choices. As a student of international relations and the law, I’m constantly learning about global causes that urgently need resources and attention. Selling artwork creates an opportunity to support these causes directly.

For this reason, half of all profits made on this website are donated to the Palestine Children’s Relief Fund and Sadagaat-USA—two organizations dedicated to addressing the ongoing injustice in Palestine and Sudan.
