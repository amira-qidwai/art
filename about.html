HI, I'M AMIRA QIDWAI

(A-mee-ra Kid-why)

I’m an artist and student at the University of Iowa. I use my experience with a variety of mediums to explore the intersection of art with international political issues, geography, and human development.

I was born and raised in Iowa City, Iowa. My roots as an Iowan, a Midwesterner, and a half White, half Pakistani woman continue to impact my artwork. Most of my early work was focused on my heritage, but through investigating Pakistani culture, I found that the connections between South Asian cultures interested me. Gradually, I became more concerned with connections and divisions between cultures on a global scale, like connections between different regional cultures or even all developing countries.

I am currently a senior at the University of Iowa where I study international relations, geographic information science (GIS), and Arabic. The lessons I learn in my studies continue to inform my artistic focuses and decisions. As a student of international relations, I am constantly learning about causes around the world that need more attention and resources. By selling artwork, I hope to raise money and awareness for some of the most dire humanitarian situations around the world. For this reason, half of the profits from all sales are donated to the Palestine Children’s Relief Fund and Sadagaat-USA, two charities dedicated to addressing the ongoing injustice in Palestine and Sudan.
