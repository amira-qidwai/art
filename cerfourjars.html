Four Jars

Not For Sale

Medium: Stoneware with Glaze

Dimensions: 5"H x 3.25"W x 3.25"D

Description: Four small, cylindrical jars, covered in neutral toned glazes. Each has an intricately carved foot and five small feet supporting it. Disc-like lids cover each jar and are complete with a finneal handle.

Further Information: One of the first assignments in the introduction to wheel-throwing course I took at my university was to throw consistently sized cylinders. I had already been throwing for a couple of years before I started this course so I choose to make the assignment a bit more challenging by throwing galleries into my consistently sizes cylinder's lips and creating a lid for each one. Then we used these cylinders to experiment with the university's glazes for the first time. I left some space on the bottom of each jar unglazed so I could see how much these new glazes would drip and eliminate the risk of them fusing to the kiln shelf.
