Bathroom Mural

Not For Sale

Medium: Wall Paint on Drywall

Dimensions: Long Walls: ~9'W x ~4'H, Short Walls: ~4'W x ~8'H

Description: I painted this mural in a bathroom for a client based on a specific wallpaper design. The mural features a ground scene with small trees, bushes, and flowers, as well as several large trees with hanging fruit. There is also an assortment of mythical creatures painted in the sky. I used a combination of 6 small cans of paint to complete this. Detailed wall paper and installation can often be more expensive than a custom mural. For a project of this size I would charge around $600 plus the cost of paint which may be less expensive than buying several rolls of wallpaper.
