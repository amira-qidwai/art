Investigating Women's Education in South Asia

Not For Sale

Medium: Newspaper, Cardstock, Marker, Watercolor Paper, and Watercolor Paint

Description: Triptych featuring three women in traditional South Asian clothign alongside a poem by Malala Yousafzai. Background is made of Urdu newspaper clipings.

Further Information: I made these three pieces during the COVID-19 Lockdown using newspaper clipings from an Urdu-langauge newspaper I picked up on Devon Street in Chicago. They are not for sale because I gave these pieces to an art show several years ago and unfortunately never got them back. But, I could likely recreate these pieces or create something similar. If you are interested, please reach out to me using the information in the contact tab.
