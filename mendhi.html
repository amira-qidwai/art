Mendhi Services

For Sale

Medium: Henna Paste or Acrylic Paint

Price: Depends on size and intricacy of design

Description: Mendhi services can look different based on the event or project. They can involve doing mendhi for an individual or a group of people with organic mendhi paste. They can also involve decorating an item or several items with mendhi designs using acrylic paint.

Further Information: I have practiced mendhi for seven years, including both traditional mendhi done on skin using mendhi/henna paste and more permanent versions of it completed with acrylic paint. I've decorated many objects using acrylic paint including textiles, plastics, and ceramics. My experience with this technique also covers a variety of styles including South Asian, Arab, and North African designs.
