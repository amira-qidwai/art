Three Handled Vase

Not For Sale

Medium: Stoneware with Glaze

Dimensions: 14"H x 8"W x 13.5"D

Description: Three-handled vase with narrow neck and wide belly shape. Base of each handle and lip of vase is decorated with mendhi designs. Rest of vase glazed in white and painted with blue leaves and pink flowers.

Further Information This vase was the centerpiece of my final project for the ceramics course I took at my university. The theme of this set was the unity of South Asia and this vase symbolizes this in a variety of ways. This vase is also the tallest thing I've ever thrown. It was thrown in two sections and connected on the wheel. I decorated the vessel with mendhi/henna designs referecing the shared usage of mendhi/henna throughout the Indian sub-continent. I painted green leaves with blue details up the sides of the vessel and pink and white iterations of the lotus flowers, jasmine flowers, and water lilies that were central to the theme of my project. Unfortunately, the whites and greens burnt off in the heat of the kiln and the final vase looks a little muddled. The glaze also bubbled up in some places creating jagged edges. Although this piece did not turn out how I expected, it was a good learning experience in throwing large and tall.
