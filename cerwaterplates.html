Water Plates

Not For Sale

Medium: Stoneware with Glaze

Dimensions: 1"H x 10"W x 0.5"D

Description: Large plates with small rounded rim. Face of plate is covered in a mix of light blue glazes. Exterior of plate is covered in a brown glaze.

Further Information: These large plates were a part of my final project in the university ceramics course I took. I created a table setting that represented the unity of the Indian sub-continent. The light blue surface of these plates represent the relationship with water that South Asian nations share. All nations in the subcontinent struggle with monsoons and flooding almost annually. Their river systems and water resources are interconnected, forcing some-level of cooperation between South Asian countries.
