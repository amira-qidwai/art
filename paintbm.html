Bathroom Mural

Not For Sale

Medium: Wall Paint on Drywall

Dimensions: Long Walls: ~9'W x ~4'H, Short Walls: ~4'W x ~8'H

Description: Mural painted across four walls of a bathroom. Features ground scene with small trees, bushes, and flower. Large trees with olive green leaves dominate mural. Trees have large pink pears hanging from them. An assortment of magical creatures fly over scene.

Further Information: I painted this mural for a client as an alternative to wallpaper. Some wallpaper rolls are so expensive that buying enough to cover a room like this would cost upwards of a thousand dollars. Instead, I studied the plates and creatures in the wallpaper this client had found and painted a mural inspired by those designs. This mural took me about 15 hours to paint. To complete something similar I would probably charge about $500-$600 plus the cost of wall paint.
