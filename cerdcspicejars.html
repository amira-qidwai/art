Decolonized Spice Jar Set

Not For Sale

Medium: Stoneware with Glaze

Dimensions: Jars size varies, ~5"H x ~3"W x ~3.75"D

Description: This collection of 24 spice jars is based off the Lenox Spice Village. The original village features 24 dutch houses and the collection here contrasts this colonial message by representing each spice with its original architectural tradition. A combination of oxides and glazes were used to decorate the outsides of these jars and the inside of each jar and lid was coated with white glaze to ensure they are food safe. This set was created as a proof of concept; it is not completely fit for purpose, but it was an exciting learning experience and something I would be interested in trying again.
