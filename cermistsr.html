Mist Spoon Rest

Not For Sale

Medium: Stoneware with Glaze

Dimensions: 1.25"H x 5.5"W x 1"D

Description: Spoon-rest plate with rim shaped into six petals. Sixth petal is pulled open to create a stable space for a cooking utensil to rest. Vessel is covered in a light green glaze with blue, pink, and white speckles flowing into the piece, creating a mist-like effect.

Further Information: This spoon rest was a prototype exploring how to include the floral rim style in shallower, plate-like vessels.
