The Developing World

For Sale

Medium: Acrylic Paint and Streched Canvas

Price: $45.00

Dimensions: 12"H x 24"W

Description: Painting of a world map with regions of the world classified as "developing" decorated with abstract floral designs. Edge of the map features gold acrylic mendhi/henna designs.

Further Information: This painting was a part of my AP Art portfolio exploring human development. Many of my works in this series focused on common aspects of culture that united developing countries and I wanted to include a piece that mapped out exactly what the developing world is. The flowers designs, bright colors, and connective chains represent developing countries' shared experience of growth.
