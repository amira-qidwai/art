Petaled Earth Strainer

Not For Sale

Medium: Stoneware with Glaze

Dimensions: 5.25"H x 8.5"W x 5"D

Description: Strainer with decoratively carved holes. Two handles are carved across from each other for easy holding. Vessel features a tan glaze with brown speckles on the top half flowing into a sage green flaze on the bottom half. Rim of strainer is fashioned into six petals.

Further Information: I use this vessel as a strainer and produce washing bowl in my kitchen. It was a prototype for berry bowls and it features one of my favorite glaze combinations. But, I likely won't sell it because all of the smallest holes were filled in with glaze and the handles are too small to be practical. Still, it was a fun and beautiful experiment.
