Two-Toned Bismallah Cup Set

Not For Sale

Medium: Stoneware with Glaze

Dimensions: 4.5"H x 3.75"W x 4"D

Description: Four cups with a tapered cyclindrical shape. Each has the first words of the Quran, "bismallah ir-rahman ir-raheem," piped up the side. Opposite side features a floral, branch-like mendhi design. Cups are dipped in a blue and green glaze that overlap and form a seafoam colored glaze over the mendhi work and Arabic calligraphy.

Further Information: I originally created nine identical cups with the same mendhi/henna inspired decoration, but after firing I realized that stacking these cups inside of one another wouldn't be practical because overtime the decorations would break off. In liue of this, I glazed five of these cups with different unique glaze combinations so they wouldn't be styled as sets. I glazed these four as a set to use in my kitchen and see if normal, practical use of cups with this design would lead to breakage.
