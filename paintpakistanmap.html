Map of Pakistan

Not For Sale

Medium: Acrylic Paint and Streched Canvas

Dimensions: 30"H x 30"W

Description: Large, square painting of a map of Pakistan in tan, dark blue, and pink tones. Stylized lettering meant to look similar to Urdu labels major cities and landmarks.

Further Information: This painting took me over a year to complete. Drawing the map as geographically accurate as possible took a great deal of math and measurements. I also individually planned and drafted each of the labels to get the lettering perfect before I painted it onto the map. It now hangs in the guest room where my grandparents stay when they visit.
