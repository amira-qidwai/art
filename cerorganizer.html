Ceramic Organizer

Not For Sale

Medium: Stoneware with Glaze

Description: A large ceramic vessel with four compartments and one lidded compartment. The organizer is decorated with green glaze and painted and carved lotus flowers.

Further Information: Some of my favorite items to build in ceramics are organizers like this piece. Large pieces like this are challenging because of their tendency to warp and crack. I built this piece twice because the first time the back wall cracked before it was even fired. There is a video of me making this organizer of the "Ceramics Growth" page and it includes clips from making this the first and second times. I use this piece in my own home to store cosmetics.
