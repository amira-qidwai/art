Mountain Scene Incense Holder

Not For Sale

Medium: Stoneware with Glaze

Dimensions: 1.25"H x 8"Dia

Description: Incense holder meant for incense cones. Cone sits at the top of a mountain at the center of the plate and smoke emanating from the cone flows down the channels on the mountain and around the five mountains at the base. Piece is covered in a light green glaze with specks of blue and yellow appearing in the carved texture of mountains.

Further Information: I made this incense holder specifically for lotus cones, which sit at the top of the center mountain. The holes and channels down the side of the center mountain allow the smoke generated by the lotus cone to flow from the top of the mountain to the valleys between the smaller mountains around the plate.
